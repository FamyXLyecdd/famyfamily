Refund Policy

Last updated: December 6, 2024

Important Notice

Famy's Family is a community platform that facilitates peer-to-peer trading. We do not sell products or services directly and do not process payments. Trades occur between individual users.

1. Platform Role

As a platform facilitator, Famy's Family:

  • Provides a space for traders to connect
  • Offers optional middleman services for secure trades
  • Does not guarantee the outcome of any trade
  • Does not handle or hold user funds (except temporarily during middleman transactions)

2. Middleman Service Disputes

If you used our middleman service and believe there was an error:

  • Open a support ticket immediately (within 24 hours)
  • Provide evidence of the agreed trade terms
  • Our staff will investigate and mediate
  • If the error was on our staff's part, we will work to make it right

3. Peer-to-Peer Trades Without Middleman

For trades conducted without using our middleman service:

  • Users trade at their own risk
  • We cannot guarantee refunds or recovery
  • We may investigate and ban scammers
  • Recovery of lost items/funds is not guaranteed

We strongly recommend always using a middleman for any trade.

4. Scam Reports

If you were scammed by another user:

  1. Document everything (screenshots, chat logs)
  2. Open a ticket in our Discord server
  3. Provide the scammer's Discord ID
  4. Our team will investigate

While we take all reports seriously and will ban verified scammers, we cannot promise recovery of your items or funds.

5. Third-Party Payments

For disputes involving payments made through third-party services (PayPal, CashApp, etc.):

  • Contact the payment provider directly
  • Refer to their refund and dispute policies
  • Famy's Family has no control over external payment systems

6. Premium/Booster Benefits

Server boosting is managed by Discord. We do not offer refunds for:

  • Discord Nitro subscriptions
  • Server boost charges
  • Any perks associated with boosting

Contact Discord support for boost-related billing issues.

7. Contact

For refund inquiries or dispute resolution, please open a support ticket in our Discord server. Do not DM staff directly unless instructed.
