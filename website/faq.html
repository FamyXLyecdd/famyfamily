Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions

What is Famy's Family?

Famy's Family is a trusted trading community on Discord. We provide a safe platform for users to trade digital goods, including in-game currencies like Robux, game accounts, and other virtual items. Our verification system and middleman services help prevent scams and ensure secure transactions.

How do I get verified?

Getting verified is simple:

  1. Join our Discord server
  2. Go to the verification channel
  3. Click the verification link or button
  4. Connect your Discord account on our website
  5. Complete the captcha verification

Note: Your Discord account must be at least 7 days old to pass verification.

Why was my verification rejected?

Verification may be rejected for several reasons:

  • Account is less than 7 days old
  • Account has no profile picture
  • You're using an alt account
  • Previous ban on another account

If you believe this is an error, please open a support ticket in the Discord server.

How does the middleman service work?

Our middleman (MM) service protects both buyers and sellers:

  1. Both parties agree to the trade terms
  2. Request a middleman in the trading channel
  3. A staff member will hold the items/funds
  4. Once both sides deliver, the MM completes the trade
  5. If there's a dispute, the MM mediates

Always use a middleman for larger trades. It's free and keeps everyone safe.

What should I do if I get scammed?

If you believe you've been scammed:

  1. Gather all evidence (screenshots, messages)
  2. Open a ticket immediately in the server
  3. Do not confront the scammer publicly
  4. Provide the scammer's Discord ID/username
  5. Wait for staff to investigate

Note: We cannot guarantee recovery of lost items if you traded without a middleman.

Can I advertise my services here?

Yes, but only in designated channels. You may post your trading offers in the appropriate trading channels. However, advertising other Discord servers, external services, or competing platforms is not allowed. Spamming or excessive self-promotion will result in warnings or bans.

How do I become a staff member?

Staff applications open periodically. Requirements typically include:

  • Active member for at least 1 month
  • Good standing with no warnings
  • Helpful attitude in the community
  • Available timezone coverage

Applications are announced in the server. Do not DM staff asking to apply.

What is BitterFamy?

BitterFamy is our custom Discord bot that powers the Famy's Family community. It handles:

  • User verification and anti-alt detection
  • Welcome messages and user guidance
  • Automated trading announcements
  • Server management and moderation
Do you have a refund policy?

Famy's Family is a platform, not a seller. We do not process payments directly. For trade-related disputes, we provide mediation but cannot guarantee refunds. If you used a middleman and there was an error on our part, contact staff immediately for resolution. See our Refund Policy for details.

Still have questions?

If you couldn't find what you're looking for, join our Discord server and open a support ticket. Our staff team is available to help you.
