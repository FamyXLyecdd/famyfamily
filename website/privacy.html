Privacy Policy

Last updated: December 6, 2024

1. Information We Collect

When you use our Services, we may collect the following information:

1.1 Information from Discord

  • Discord User ID
  • Username and discriminator
  • Avatar (public profile picture)
  • Account creation date
  • Server membership status

1.2 Information We Do NOT Collect

  • Private messages or DMs
  • Email addresses (unless voluntarily provided)
  • Payment information (handled by third parties)
  • Real names or physical addresses

2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

  • Verify your identity and prevent alt accounts
  • Provide access to community features
  • Enforce community rules and Terms of Service
  • Improve our Services and user experience
  • Communicate important updates

3. Data Storage and Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your information. Data is stored securely and access is limited to authorized personnel only. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information only:

  • With your consent
  • To comply with legal obligations
  • To protect our rights or safety
  • With service providers who assist our operations

5. Cookies and Tracking

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Remember your preferences
  • Analyze website traffic
  • Display relevant advertisements

You can control cookie settings through your browser preferences.

6. Third-Party Services

Our Services integrate with third parties including:

  • Discord - Authentication and community platform
  • Monetag - Advertising network
  • Vercel - Website hosting

These services have their own privacy policies which we encourage you to review.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to your data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Opt out of non-essential data collection
  • Withdraw consent at any time

To exercise these rights, contact our staff through Discord.

8. Children's Privacy

Our Services are not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If we discover such data has been collected, we will delete it promptly.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify users of significant changes through our Discord server. Continued use of our Services after changes constitutes acceptance.

10. Contact Us

For privacy-related questions or requests, please contact our staff through the Discord server or open a support ticket.
